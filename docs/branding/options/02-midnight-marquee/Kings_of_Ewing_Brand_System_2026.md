# Kings of Ewing — Brand System (2026) · Midnight Marquee

A warm, stage-lights identity that feels live and inviting without leaning retro.
A flowing script wordmark sits under a row of marquee bulbs, with a condensed
sans subline.

## Logo

**Primary logo** — `Kings_of_Ewing_Logo_2026.svg` (for dark backgrounds) and
`Kings_of_Ewing_Logo_2026_light.svg` (for light backgrounds). "Kings" in script
with "OF EWING" spaced beneath, topped by marquee bulbs.

**Icon / submark** — `Kings_of_Ewing_Icon.svg`. A KOE monogram on a midnight tile
with a marquee-bulb row. Use for profile avatars, favicons, and stickers. The
square crops cleanly to a circle for Instagram.

### Clear space and sizing
Keep clear space around the logo equal to the bulb diameter on all sides. The
script needs room to breathe — don't crowd it. Minimum legible width for the
primary logo is about 140px (script detail); below that, use the icon.

### Don'ts
Don't recolor outside the palette, don't tilt or arc the wordmark, don't add
extra glow/shadow to the bulbs, and don't substitute a different script face.

## Color palette

| Role | Name | Hex |
|------|------|-----|
| Primary / ground | Midnight Blue | `#10243f` |
| Accent / bulbs | Marquee Amber | `#f0a830` |
| Light / paper | Blush Cream | `#f5e9df` |
| Amber on light bg | Deep Amber | `#c8851f` |

Midnight is the signature ground; amber is the spark (bulbs, subline, links).
On light backgrounds, swap amber for Deep Amber so it stays legible.

## Typography

**Display — Pacifico.** The script logo face and the occasional warm headline.
Google Fonts: `Pacifico`.

**Subheads / labels — Oswald.** Condensed sans for dates, venues, buttons, and
the "OF EWING" subline. Google Fonts: `Oswald` (700).

**Body — Inter.** Clean humanist sans for paragraphs, captions, and email copy.
Google Fonts: `Inter` (400 and 600).

```
@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Pacifico&family=Oswald:wght@500;700&family=Inter:wght@400;600&display=swap');
```

file). Can be converted to outlined vector paths for print/offline use on request.
