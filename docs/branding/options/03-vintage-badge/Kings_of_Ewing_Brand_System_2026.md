# Kings of Ewing — Brand System (2026) · Vintage Badge

A warm, hand-stamped emblem that roots the band in Evanston. A circular seal with
arched lettering, a center star, and a thin inner keyline — built to look great on
merch, stamps, stickers, and tees.

## Logo

**Standard badge** — `Kings_of_Ewing_Badge_2026.svg`. The self-contained emblem;
works on any light or photo background.

**Dark badge** — `Kings_of_Ewing_Badge_2026_dark.svg`. Forest-ground variant for
dark backgrounds.

**Horizontal lockup** — `Kings_of_Ewing_Logo_2026_horizontal.svg`. Badge beside a
stacked "KINGS / OF EWING" wordmark, for site headers and other wide spaces where
a pure circle is awkward.

**Icon / favicon** — `Kings_of_Ewing_Icon.svg`. A simplified rust disc, cream
keyline, and center star — legible down to favicon size where the arched text
isn't.

### Clear space and sizing
Keep clear space around the badge equal to the width of the rust outer ring.
The full badge stays legible down to about 48px; below that, use the icon.
For wide layouts, prefer the horizontal lockup over shrinking the circle.

### Don'ts
Don't recolor outside the palette, don't remove the inner keyline, don't replace
the star with other art, and don't set the arched text in a different face.

## Color palette

| Role | Name | Hex |
|------|------|-----|
| Primary / outer ring | Rust | `#b5532a` |
| Secondary | Forest Green | `#2f5d3a` |
| Accent / star | Mustard | `#d9a441` |
| Ground / paper | Cream | `#f2e8d5` |
| Dark ground | Deep Forest | `#1f3d28` |

Rust and Forest carry the type; Mustard is the spark (star, dots); Cream is the
paper ground.

## Typography

**Display — Alfa Slab One.** The heavy slab used in the badge arc and the
"KINGS" wordmark. Google Fonts: `Alfa Slab One`.

**Subheads / labels — Oswald.** Condensed sans for the "EVANSTON · ILLINOIS"
curve, dates, venues, and buttons. Google Fonts: `Oswald` (500 and 700).

**Body — Inter.** Clean humanist sans for paragraphs, captions, and email copy.
Google Fonts: `Inter` (400 and 600).

```
@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Alfa+Slab+One&family=Oswald:wght@500;700&family=Inter:wght@400;600&display=swap');
```

## Notes
