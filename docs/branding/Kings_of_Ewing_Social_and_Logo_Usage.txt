TAGGING A SHOW POST (Instagram)
-------------------------------

1. PHOTO TAGS — tag the band members and the venue/organizer directly in
   the photo. This surfaces the post on each member's "Tagged" tab and on
   the venue's, which significantly expands reach:

     @acri.jim, @professorfreak, @jhwestwood, @mattstork1, plus the
     venue/organizer handle (e.g. @ridgevilleparks).

2. CAPTION MENTIONS — repeat the same handles at the bottom of the caption:

     @acri.jim @professorfreak @jhwestwood @mattstork1 @venuehandle

3. MAILING-LIST CTA — include the signup line in every post:

     📬 Never miss a show — join our mailing list at kingsofewing.com

4. HASHTAGS — always lead with #KingsOfEwing, then mix in location and
   event tags, e.g.:

     #KingsOfEwing #Evanston #LiveMusic #ConcertsOnTheRidge

LOGO USAGE
----------

  Use the official logo from docs/branding/Kings_of_Ewing_Logo.png.
  Keep clear space around the logo and don't stretch, recolor, or add
  effects. On busy photos, place it on a solid or semi-transparent panel
  so it stays legible.
