FULL BIO
--------
Kings of Ewing is a five-piece band from Evanston, Illinois. All five live on the same block on Ewing Ave — hence the name. They started in 2023 to play their own block party, liked it so much they never stopped.

By day they hold down regular jobs. By night they play the music they love: rock, pop, folk, blues, and soul from the '60s to today, from Beatles classics and Allman Brothers jams to Tom Petty, Chappell Roan, and everything in between. All five pull double duty — four sing lead, and between them they cover horn and rhythm guitar (Miles), keys (Matt), lead guitar (Drew), bass (Jack), and drums (Jim). They know how to read a room, keep the dance floor moving, and get the crowd singing along.

The band plays weddings, block parties, fundraisers, bars, and backyard gatherings throughout North Chicagoland.

No rock star egos. No tour bus. Just five friends from the same block who happen to make a really good racket.
