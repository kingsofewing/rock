Turf Toe

No push-off loading: skip standing climbs, calf raises, squats/lunges/deadlifts for now. Keep any big-toe stretch slow and pain-free.

Form First

Coming off a layoff, start lighter than feels necessary. Leave 1–2 reps in the tank. Quality of movement beats load.

Progress

Add a little to the long ride each week. Once 75 min feels easy, add load or sets to strength before chasing more time.

Flex It

Tight on time? Cut isolation moves to 2 sets (~35 min). Toe flares? Swap the run for rowing or an easy spin.
General training guidance, not medical advice — if the toe is painful when walking, get a PT or doctor's clearance before resuming impact work.
