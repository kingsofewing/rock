----------------

When posting about a Kings of Ewing show or event, please tag the band and
its members as follows:

    Photo tags surface the post on each member's "Tagged" tab and significantly
    expand reach, so both caption mentions and photo tags are appreciated.

  YouTube
    Mention in video title or description: Kings of Ewing
    Link to the channel: youtube.com/@KingsofEwing

  General
    Use the hashtag #KingsOfEwing in any social post.
    Link to the website when possible: KingsOfEwing.com
